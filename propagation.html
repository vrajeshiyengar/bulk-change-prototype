3Propagating the change

Rippling is the system of record. A change commits once it's approved and its effective date arrives. Each downstream system syncs as its own job. A third-party failure never blocks or rolls back the Rippling change, and never blocks payroll.

How we know which systems to update

Each integration subscribes to profile fields. The changed fields, crossed with those subscriptions and the people who have an account in each app, give exactly the Integrated Apps list on the Impact & approvals step. The IT owner sets the mapping up once; the HR admin never does.

The hard parts

Payroll cutoffFirst-partyLate approvals choose next run with retro pay, or off-cycle, before submit.
Location changesFirst-partyTax state, leave policy and benefits region move with the work location.
Dynamic segmentsFirst-partyMembership is frozen at submit, so approvers sign off on named people.
Rate limitsThird-partyBatch and throttle; show an ETA instead of an error.
App rejects a valueThird-partyRoute the app's own message to its owner. Rippling's value stands.
Irreversible effectsThird-partyAccess removals and email renames are framed as losses and wait for the effective date.
