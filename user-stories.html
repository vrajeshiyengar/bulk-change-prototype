SegmentsTargeting the wrong people, silentlyOne condition builder, a live count with a labelled delta, a "why included" sample
FieldsPicking the wrong field among hundreds that look alikeSearch first, descriptions, last-used recency, locked fields shown with the reason
ValuesErrors found after submit, not while editingOne Change values tool with a preview, per-cell reasons, approval needs shown per row, undo
Review & confirmConfirming numbers nobody checkedA deterministic change list, an AI summary that cites it, every count drillable to people
Impact & approvalsDownstream surprises: payroll timing, access lossA flowchart of every affected system and the decisions only the admin can make
Submit & trackA third-party failure undoing an HR decisionRippling commits first; each app syncs on its own and never rolls the record back

4How we will know it works

CorrectnessPost-commit correctionsShare of bulk changes needing a fix or reversal within 30 days. Target: under 2%.
SpeedTime to submitFrom draft to submit for a 400-person comp cycle. Target: under 45 minutes, including review.
TrustDrill-downs before confirmShare of submitters who open at least one count on Review & confirm. A signal they verify, not skim.
ApprovalsApprover turnaroundMedian hours from request to decision, and the share of rows sent back.
DownstreamSync failures resolvedMedian time from a failed app sync to resolved, with zero Rippling rollbacks.
AdoptionBulk change vs CSV importShare of multi-employee edits made through bulk change instead of one-off imports or support tickets.

5Out of scope, and open questions

ItemPosition
Moves to another countryOut of scope. They change legal entity, so they go to the international transfer flow; bulk change flags and moves those rows.
Who may bulk-edit compensationOpen. Assumed: a permission per field group, with self-approval limits per admin set in Settings › Approvals.
Two drafts touching the same personWarn while drafting; whichever is submitted second shows a conflict. Locking at draft time is rejected because it blocks planning.
Dynamic segments after submitMembership is computed at submit, so approvers sign off on named people. New joiners are not added later.
Bulk change by APILater. The same validation and approval engine would sit behind it.
Editing after approvalNot allowed. A reversal or a follow-up draft keeps the audit trail clean.
